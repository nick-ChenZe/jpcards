# Product Brief (v0.2)

## 1) Product Vision

Build a Japanese learning app that helps users keep a sustainable daily study habit and feel measurable progress without getting overwhelmed.

## 2) Problem Statement

Many self-learners start with enthusiasm but quit because:

- they do not know what to study each day,
- they forget learned vocabulary quickly,
- they cannot track practical progress,
- content feels either too easy or too difficult.

## 3) Target Users (Locked for V1)

- Primary: Chinese-speaking users who want to improve Japanese speaking and vocabulary for real-life communication.
- Secondary: learners who need a guided and sustainable daily study routine.

## 4) Value Proposition (Locked for V1)

- Help users learn Japanese more naturally through AI-guided practice.
- Reinforce memory with a scientific review method.
- Reduce daily study friction with a mobile-first, easy-to-start flow.
- Allow users to bring and configure their own AI Provider on client side for flexibility and control.

## 5) Product Positioning Decision

- Locked Direction: **Option B (AI coach + flashcards)**
- Why:
  - Matches the core promise: natural language learning + scientific memory.
  - Better long-term differentiation for a launch-ready product.
  - Supports future expansion to speaking-focused flows.

## 6) V1 Positioning Statement

An AI-assisted Japanese learning app for Chinese-speaking learners, combining daily flashcard study, short AI explanations, and memory-science review to improve speaking and vocabulary confidence.

## 7) Success Criteria (v0.2)

- Daily usability: users complete at least 4 sessions per week on average.
- AI value: users actively use AI explanation in study sessions.
- Learning quality: review accuracy trend improves within first 14 days.
- AI flexibility: users can complete AI-assisted learning with their configured Provider successfully.

## 8) Out of Scope for V1

- Full grammar curriculum system
- Real-time speaking evaluation
- Multi-language UI support
- Social community features
- Custom user-created cards
- JLPT level selection in onboarding
